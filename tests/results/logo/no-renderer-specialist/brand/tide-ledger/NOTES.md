# Tide Ledger icon

The tide curve represents changing water height; two ledger rules represent recorded observations. The shorter final rule leaves an open, unfinished-entry silhouette. Deep teal (#11666B) connects the mark to fieldwork and water. No wordmark or backdrop is required.

Original, manually authored vector geometry; no external artwork, fonts, raster content, or image generation. Editable paths are named in the SVG. The 32 × 32 canvas is transparent, with a single fixed stroke color. Change the group's stroke to adapt the mark for dark backgrounds or monochrome use. Product context comes from the repository README.md; no existing identity was supplied. This is a newly created concept, not an approved existing identity.

The three paths use a 3-unit stroke (3 px at native size; 2.25 px at 24 px). A preliminary full-width final ledger line was shortened in the final source to reduce the block-like footprint and give the log metaphor an open entry. This refinement is based on geometry, not a rendered comparison.
