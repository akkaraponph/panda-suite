# Saving a draft

Evidence status: **Documented from supplied evidence; not fully verified.** These steps are based only on the product owner’s note in `supplied-evidence.md`. Browser validation and screenshots were unavailable.

1. In the draft form, enter your title in **Request title**.
2. Select **Save draft**.
3. Check for **Draft saved: ** followed by the title you entered. For example, entering `Office supplies` should show `Draft saved: Office supplies`.

The supplied note identifies that message as the successful-save confirmation. It does not establish what happens if the message is absent, whether drafts persist after reopening, or how to access the form. Delete, publish, email, and administration procedures are not evidenced.
